## 1. Executive Summary

Phase V5.5 integrates an end-to-end travel consultation and inquiry workflow into the JMT Travels luxury travel portal. Recognizing that JMT Travels operates as a high-touch, bespoke travel agency rather than an automated OTA (Online Travel Agency) with direct GDS inventory, this phase establishes a consultation intake and management pipeline.

Customers can now submit structured hotel consultation requests (`/hotels` and `/account/travel-requests`) and flight ticketing inquiries (`/flights` and `/account/travel-requests`). Customers can track their travel requests in real time via the authenticated portal at `/account/travel-requests`, inspect milestone timelines and concierge notes at `/account/travel-requests/:id`, cancel pending requests with reason auditing, and view consultation items alongside tour bookings and visa applications within the unified **My Trips** portal (`/account/my-trips`).

All implementations adhere strictly to the project's security foundations: zero IDOR vulnerabilities, strict token-derived customer ownership, rate limiting (15 submissions / 15 minutes), comprehensive input validation (no past dates, date order enforcement, passenger bounds), customer DTO sanitization (redacting internal staff notes and storage keys), and honest user interface copy (no fabricated PNRs, no fake live seat counts, and no false claims of instant booking confirmation).

## 3. Hotel Inquiry Modal & Flow

**Modal Entry Points:**
- **Hotels Catalog:** Clicking `"Enquire Hotel →"` on any property card in `/hotels` opens the travel inquiry modal prefilled with that hotel's name, city, district, and current search dates (`hotelSearchState.checkIn`, `hotelSearchState.checkOut`).
- **Empty Search States:** When destination filters yield 0 results, a `"Request Hotel Consultation →"` CTA is presented to capture custom requests.
- **Customer Portal:** Clicking `"+ New Request"` on `/account/travel-requests` or `"Hotel Consultation"` in Customer Dashboard Quick Actions triggers the intake modal.

**Intake Form Fields & Capabilities:**
- **Destination / City:** Required (minimum 2 characters, max 120 characters).
- **Check-in & Check-out Dates:** Date pickers enforcing `YYYY-MM-DD` formatting, rejecting past dates and inverted date ranges (`checkOutDate >= checkInDate`).
- **Rooms & Guests:** Adults (1–20), Children (0–20), and Rooms (1–10) with validation bounds.
- **Preferred Accommodation:** Optional property name or preferred chain (e.g. Al Bustan Palace, Chedi Muscat).
- **Room Preference:** Category preferences (Standard, Deluxe, Sea View Suite, Villa, Connecting Rooms).
- **Budget Range:** Indicative budget (Budget-Friendly, Premium Comfort, Ultra-Luxury).
- **Special Requirements:** Free-text textarea for dietary, accessibility, or anniversary requests (max 1000 characters).
- **Contact Preference:** WhatsApp or Email.

**Submission Flow & Guest Fallback:**
- **Authenticated Customers:** Posts directly to `POST /api/account/travel-requests` with Bearer JWT. Receives 201 Created with generated reference (e.g. `JMT-H-477905`), renders an immediate success confirmation with direct links to view the request in the customer portal (`/account/travel-requests`), and logs audit action `CREATE_TRAVEL_REQUEST`.
- **Guest / Unauthenticated Visitors:** Gracefully falls back to `/api/feedback` with structured inquiry metadata, prompts the visitor with an option to sign in or create an account to track the consultation live, and provides a direct WhatsApp concierge handoff link.

## 4. Flight Inquiry Modal & Flow

**Modal Entry Points:**
- **Flights Search Results:** Clicking `"Request Ticket →"` on any flight card in `/flights` triggers the modal prefilled with route (`fromAirport`, `toAirport`), airline (`Oman Air`, `SalamAir`, etc.), flight number, and departure dates.
- **Empty Search States:** When route queries return no matching scheduled flights, a `"Request Flight Consultation →"` button enables custom itinerary requests.
- **Customer Portal:** Direct button on `/account/travel-requests` and Customer Dashboard Quick Actions.

**Intake Form Fields & Capabilities:**
- **Origin & Destination:** IATA or city names (minimum 2 characters). Validates that origin and destination are distinct (`origin.toLowerCase() !== destination.toLowerCase()`).
- **Departure & Return Dates:** Enforces valid future date formats. For round-trip journeys, requires `returnDate >= departureDate`.
- **Trip Type:** Supports `round-trip`, `one-way`, and `multi-city`.
- **Passengers:** Adults (1–20), Children (0–20), and Infants (0–10).
- **Cabin Preference:** Economy, Premium Economy, Business Class, First Class.
- **Preferred Airline:** Optional airline preference (e.g. Oman Air, Qatar Airways, Emirates).
- **Special Requirements:** Meal preference, extra baggage, wheelchair assistance, seat assignment notes (max 1000 characters).

**Submission Flow:**
- Validates all fields client-side and server-side.
- Generates a unique reference starting with `JMT-F-` (e.g. `JMT-F-760814`).
- Immediately reflects the flight inquiry in the customer's portal and logs audit event `CREATE_TRAVEL_REQUEST`.

## 7. Customer Cancellation Workflow

Endpoint: `PATCH /api/account/travel-requests/:id/cancel`
- **Ownership Verification:** Strictly enforces that `inquiry.userId === req.user.id`. Cross-customer cancellation attempts trigger `403 FORBIDDEN` and security audit logging.
- **Status Safeguards:** Requests already in `CANCELLED`, `CLOSED`, or `RESOLVED` cannot be cancelled again; returns `400 REQUEST_ALREADY_CLOSED`.
- **Reason Capture:** Customers are prompted for a cancellation reason (e.g. "Found alternative dates", "Trip postponed"). The cancellation note is appended to `staffResponse` for operational clarity.
- **Audit Logging:** Logs audit event `CANCEL_TRAVEL_REQUEST` with reference and inquiry type.
- **UI State Update:** Instantly updates card badge, disables cancel button, and reflects cancelled status across My Trips and Customer Dashboard metrics.

## 11. Data Model & Architecture Decisions

1. **Model Reuse over Duplication:**
   Rather than introducing new fragmented schemas, the existing `ContactInquiry` model was extended. This maintains backward compatibility with legacy general contact submissions while providing a first-class schema for customer travel requests.
2. **Schema Enhancements in `backend/models/ContactInquiry.js`:**
   - `reference`: Indexed unique human-readable string (`JMT-H-XXXXXX`, `JMT-F-XXXXXX`).
   - `userId`: Indexed customer ID linking inquiry to user profile.
   - `type`: Enum `['GENERAL', 'HOTEL_INQUIRY', 'FLIGHT_INQUIRY', 'TOUR_INQUIRY', 'VISA_INQUIRY']`.
   - `status`: Enum `['NEW', 'RECEIVED', 'IN_REVIEW', 'CUSTOMER_ACTION_REQUIRED', 'QUOTED', 'CONFIRMED', 'CANCELLED', 'CLOSED', 'RESOLVED']`.
   - `details`: Mixed schema object storing typed consultation parameters.
   - `staffResponse`: Customer-visible reply from travel specialists.
   - `adminNotes`: Internal staff notes strictly hidden from customers.
   - `resolved`, `resolvedBy`, `resolvedAt`: Resolution audit tracking.
3. **Customer DTO Sanitization Function (`formatCustomerTravelRequestDTO`):**
   - Strictly sanitizes every inquiry before sending it across the network.
   - Redacts `adminNotes`, `internalNotes`, `staffNotes`, `storageKey`, `filePath`, `diskPath`, `_id`, `__v`.
   - Derives a customer-safe timeline and next-action recommendation.

## 12. Security & IDOR Defense

1. **Authentication Enforcement:**
   All `/api/account/travel-requests` endpoints require a verified Bearer JWT (`authenticate` middleware). Unauthenticated calls return `401 UNAUTHORIZED`.
2. **Strict Identity Derivation:**
   Customer identity is derived exclusively from `req.user.id`.
3. **Parameter Override Defense:**
   - Any `userId` supplied in the request body is ignored; the server unconditionally writes `req.user.id`.
   - Any `?userId=` query parameter is ignored; queries are unconditionally scoped to `filter.userId = req.user.id`.
4. **Cross-Customer Isolation (IDOR Defense):**
   - Attempting to read another customer's request (`GET /api/account/travel-requests/:id`) returns `403 FORBIDDEN` and logs `SUSPICIOUS_IDOR_TRAVEL_REQUEST_VIEW_ATTEMPT`.
   - Attempting to cancel another customer's request (`PATCH /api/account/travel-requests/:id/cancel`) returns `403 FORBIDDEN` and logs `SUSPICIOUS_IDOR_TRAVEL_REQUEST_CANCEL_ATTEMPT`.
5. **Staff Access Control:**
   Staff and administrators (`STAFF`, `ADMIN`, `SUPER_ADMIN`) are authorized to inspect inquiries across customers for legitimate concierge fulfillment.

---

## 13. Input Validation & Abuse Prevention

1. **Rate Limiting:**
   Submissions are throttled by `submissionLimiter` (15 requests per 15-minute window per IP). Exceeding this limit returns `429 RATE_LIMITED`.
2. **Date Sanity Checks:**
   - Format: Strict `YYYY-MM-DD` regex validation.
   - Past Date Rejection: Travel dates before current date are rejected with `400 PAST_TRAVEL_DATE`.
   - Chronological Order: Check-out date before check-in date or return flight date before departure date is rejected with `400 INVALID_DATE_ORDER`.
3. **Route Sanity Checks:**
   - Flight origin and destination cannot be identical; rejected with `400 SAME_ORIGIN_DESTINATION`.
4. **Passenger & Room Bounds:**
   - Adults: 1 to 20.
   - Children: 0 to 20.
   - Infants: 0 to 10.
   - Hotel Rooms: 1 to 10.
   - Out-of-bounds values return `400 INVALID_GUEST_COUNT` or `400 INVALID_PASSENGER_COUNT`.
5. **String Sanitization:**
   - Text fields are trimmed and truncated (e.g. destination max 120 chars, special requirements max 1000 chars) to prevent payload bloat.

## 18. Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Same Origin and Destination:** Flight requests specifying origin identical to destination are rejected immediately.
- **Same-Day Hotel Stay:** Allows same-day check-in and check-out for day-use bookings.
- **Non-Existent Inquiry ID:** Querying an invalid or non-existent inquiry returns `404 NOT_FOUND` without leaking system stack traces.
- **Closed Request Cancellation:** Cancelling an already `CANCELLED`, `CLOSED`, or `RESOLVED` request returns `400 REQUEST_ALREADY_CLOSED`.
- **Malformed Date Strings:** Reject non-date strings or invalid leap days (e.g. `2026-02-30`) via combined regex and `Date.getTime()` checks.
